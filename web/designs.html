Concept A · built

Floating assistant

A red "Ask Price" pill sits in the corner of every page. It opens a compact chat panel that shows its work while it searches and reads pages, then answers with linked sources. This is the most familiar pattern and it ships as one script tag.

Recommended first

Why

  • One <script> tag on the WordPress theme; no layout changes.
  • Works on department sites too (ece, mech, bme…).
  • Shadow DOM keeps the theme's CSS from leaking in.
  • Expand button turns it into Concept C for long chats.

Watch out for

  • Corner widgets are easy to ignore, so the first visit shows a short greeting bubble.
  • The theme's back-to-top button needs to move up about 70px.
  • Goes full-screen on phones.

Turn the hero into the front door: "Ask anything about Price Engineering." Answers drop down as a card with sources, and "Continue in chat" hands off to the panel. The nav's magnifying glass opens the same experience on every page, replacing keyword search with answers.

Concept C

Side drawer: a guided assistant

Conversation states

Trust comes from showing the work

For a university site, the assistant should never feel like it's guessing. It shows what it's checking, links every claim to a page, and hands off to a person when the site doesn't have the answer.
